# Ícone da Organiza

Gerado com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`. Exportações PNG feitas com `sips`; favicon ICO contém os PNG de 16, 32 e 48 px.

Fonte: `frontend/public/icons/organiza-source.png`.
Apple Touch Icon: 180 px. PWA: 192 e 512 px, com uma entrada maskable de 512 px. O fundo opaco preenche todo o quadrado e o emblema mantém margem para o recorte do sistema.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished square PWA app icon for Organiza, a Portuguese family tasks and earnings organizer. A beautiful minimal bold emblem combining a little home silhouette with a checkmark, warm and welcoming, refined rounded geometry. Solid pastel rose background #e6b8c5 filling the entire square edge to edge, dark plum #432b39 emblem and small cream highlight. Flat graphic design, crisp edges, striking readable silhouette at 32 pixels. Center the entire emblem within the central 60 percent of canvas, generous maskable safe area. No text, letters, mockup, presentation board, surrounding border, watermark, shadows or extra icons. Single square production-ready icon.
